# Data source and field notes

Source report: Government of India, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, *Rural Health Statistics 2018–19*.
Official PDF: https://www.mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Final%20RHS%202018-19_0.pdf
Catalogue: https://www.data.gov.in/catalog/rural-health-statistics-2019

The infrastructure percentage columns were transcribed from the State/UT table in the assigned project report's Appendix A, based on MoHFW Table 8. PHC doctor shortfall counts and required posts come from MoHFW Table 19. CHC specialist shortfall counts and required posts come from MoHFW Table 28.

Staffing shortfall percentages are derived as 100 × shortfall count / required posts. Source `*` surplus is encoded as zero shortfall where reported. Chandigarh has no rural facilities; Daman & Diu has no rural CHC, so those feature values are missing and the corresponding rows are not included in complete-case clustering. All records are historical 2019 reference data.
